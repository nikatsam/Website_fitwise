# Fitwise — Editorial SEO, Gaps and Growth Rules

## Strategy: rank by solving the decision better

Core clusters are **workspace: desk↔monitor fit** and **bedroom: bed↔room fit**. Expand first to workspace accessories/clearances and bedroom furniture/clearances, then dining, living, appliances, storage, home gym, access/delivery and other adjacent fit problems, conditional on organic/search evidence. Avoid drifting into a generic calculator directory.

### What earns a distinct URL

All gates required:

1. A distinct question/decision/search intent, not just a number/wording/unit permutation.
2. Substantial original utility: scaled diagram, computed fit matrix, defined clearances, user scenarios or inverse search, based on validated measurements.
3. Real or plausibly demonstrated search demand: search result evidence, keyword dataset or actual Search Console queries; record source/date rather than invent monthly volumes.
4. SERP advantage: _specific evidence of a gap_, e.g. competing pages omit desk-depth/stand footprints, UK vs US standard, bedroom furniture or door swing. Capture URL/domain, observation date, gap and proposed improvement.
5. Cross-linkable to 2+ appropriate indexable pages; one substantive source/methodology reference where data-driven.
6. Editorial quality review. If any gate fails, put the case in a matrix/hub instead of an indexable thin page.

### Page brief before writing

Complete `templates/SEO_PAGE_BRIEF.md`: audience problem; primary search intent; top related questions; SERP observations; evidence confidence; verified dimensions/standards; direct answer; diagram design; page schema; internal links; content maintenance expectations; differentiation; risk/caveats.

### Common page outline

H1 → direct answer → SVG scale diagram and fallback numbers → assumptions + markets → interactive fit check (optional) → alternatives/matrix → sources/methodology → truly related pages.

- Do **not** gate key content behind script execution, accordions that never render static HTML or sign-in.
- Titles should naturally name the decision, not promise perfection or fabricate accuracy. Example: `Two 27-inch Monitors: What Desk Width Do You Need? | Fitwise`.
- Important heading and URL keywords occur naturally. No fixed keyword density, minimum word count, or systematic date-freshness manipulation.
- Include precise safety caveat for variable items: overall frame, bezel, feet/base, cable space and manufacturer variation. Nominal mattress size ≠ bed frame outer size; physical fit ≠ comfortable or safe ergonomic fit.
- For comparisons, show assumptions on both sides and a decision matrix. For geo-specific beds, identify size standards visibly.
- Factual citations should resolve to named manufacturer/standards/credible guidance where available. Avoid invented sources or unsourced numeric claims.
- Do not assert empirical rankings/traffic figures without source, measurement method, region and observation date.

### Expansion decision procedure (every 28 days when data exists)

1. Export or review Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster + Yandex Webmaster page/query data, each separately. Do not assume engines report identical metrics.
2. Segment by `cluster` and `pageFamily` and compare 28-day periods; mark seasonality and sample size.
3. Prioritize technical indexability errors; then pages on positions ~5–20 with proven impressions; then distinct long-tail intent gaps.
4. Update winning pages before cloning variants. Publish 3–8 concentrated high-value pages per winning cluster when evidence permits, not dozens of thin permutations.
5. Retest effect after 28–56 days, note change in impressions/CTR/clicks, and retain/merge/iterate.

### KPI tiers

- **Readiness**: intended indexable URLs built, sitemap accurate, no broken links or invalid canonical, published page response 200.
- **Discovery**: indexed vs submitted, query impressions, distinct queries, average position by query group, crawler issues.
- **Growth**: organic clicks/CTR per engine and cluster, top landing pages, non-brand vs brand queries, pages with incremental traffic.
- **Value**: solved fit checks, task completion, useful related navigation and meaningful return visits, measured privacy-sensitively.

Traffic projections in strategy conversations are **scenario models**, not official Google statistics and not contractual milestones. A well-optimized site can remain at low traffic when demand, competition or authority conditions don't cooperate.

### Continuous editorial health

Audit and update critical dataset entries when physical standards/manufacturer specs change; keep provenance and `significantlyModifiedOn` honest. Remove dead/duplicated/outdated pages with proper redirects. Avoid broad AI-generated topical sprawl and pseudo-expert claims. Link to sources where practical; original geometric calculation and visual evidence should be the defining value.

**Official policy:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
