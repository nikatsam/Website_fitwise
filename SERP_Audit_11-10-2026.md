## Executive Summary

The site's strongest search opportunity is its exact-fit utility: people search for a particular object and constraint, such as a sofa through a doorway, two monitors on a desk, or a bed in a measured room. Localized SERP sampling found direct calculators, room planners, retailers and measurement guides for these needs. The existing tool families already cover most of them, so the first move should be to improve the matching existing pages and their internal links, not create a large set of near-duplicate URLs.

The general “will furniture fit in my room?” query is also directly contested by multi-object room planners and several fit-check microsites, including a result titled “Will It Fit? 3D Room & Furniture Layout Planner.” FitWise should distinguish the one-object dimensional check from a draggable floor-plan planner and use the FitWise brand in title/snippet context rather than relying on the generic phrase alone.

Two additions have the clearest SERP evidence:

1. Reconsider the existing noindex candidate `/bedroom/what-bed-fits-in-10x10-room/`. Google autocomplete in the US suggested 10x10, 10x11, 10x12, 12x12 and 9x9 variants, plus “can a full size bed fit in a 10x10 room.” Keep the content scenario-led and evidence-backed; do not generate a page for every room size. The related 10x12 route should only be indexed if it contributes a distinct, useful comparison.
2. Build a source-backed local mattress-size reference for markets not covered by the two indexable US/UK references, or one genuinely useful international comparison page. The sampled AU, CA, NZ, Ireland and South Africa SERPs prominently returned local mattress-size charts and local terminology. Their values and labels differ, so this is a data-validation project, not a translation exercise.

A third, conditional opportunity is an informational furniture-delivery measurement guide. The SERPs show a distinct “how do I measure this sofa/fridge for delivery?” need alongside calculators. It must explain the measurement process and the limitations of straight-through geometry without claiming to simulate turns, tilting or three-dimensional handling unless the product actually supports those calculations.

**No search-volume or ranking claims are made.** Google Search Console query/impression data and a keyword-volume/difficulty provider were unavailable. Google autocomplete is demand-language discovery, not volume evidence. SERP presence does not prove that a phrase has meaningful demand or that a new page will rank quickly.

## Method and Confidence

- Inventoried the 42 current indexable sitemap URLs and mapped each to its page family and likely intent.
- Captured 43 primary DuckDuckGo HTML result samples with market/language parameters: four recurring query themes across seven locales (28), one market-specific specialist query in each of those seven locales, and the four core themes in India and Singapore (8). Additional targeted result inspections covered furniture routes, appliances, desk/monitor fit, pool tables, bedroom layouts, mattress sizes, storage, garden structures, outdoor dining, hot tubs, TV stands, home-gym racks and garage fit.
- Retrieved DuckDuckGo HTML through the Jina Reader text proxy because direct DuckDuckGo requests were rate-limited in this environment. Locale codes were `us-en`, `uk-en`, `ca-en`, `au-en`, `nz-en`, `ie-en`, `za-en`, `in-en` and `sg-en`. These settings request localized results; they do not recreate a searcher's exact city, device, history, or signed-in/personalized results.
- Queried Google's public autocomplete endpoint for six seed phrases across US, UK, Canada and Australia. The suggestions are recorded as query variants, not as “People also ask” results, search volume or ranking data.
- Direct Google SERP access was blocked in this environment. Bing HTML/RSS was also tested but returned semantically inconsistent result sets, so it was not used as ranking evidence.
- Result titles and snippets are a dated SERP snapshot. Snippets can be truncated or generated, and some returned calculator microsites require manual page-quality and factuality review before being used as benchmarks.
- The audit covers nine market settings, not every English-using country/territory or every city. India and Singapore are included as additional English-language settings; other markets such as the Philippines, Nigeria, Kenya, Malaysia, Hong Kong and Jamaica remain unsampled.
- Search Console is not connected/configured for query analysis. There is no verified position, impression, click-through-rate, query-volume, keyword-difficulty or conversion data in this report.

**Interpretation:** “High / medium priority” below is a qualitative product-fit and SERP-opportunity judgment, not a volume or probability estimate. Validate it with Search Console and a repeatable SERP/keyword dataset before committing to more indexable URLs.

## SERP Patterns

### Furniture Delivery and Doorways

For “sofa fit through doorway calculator,” US and UK samples returned multiple exact-title calculators, including [AI Room Styler](https://airoomstyler.com/tools/sofa-fit-calculator), [ItemFits](https://itemfits.com/fit/door/couch), [MiniWebTool](https://miniwebtool.com/couch-fit-calculator/), [BuildWise Calculator](https://www.buildwisecalculator.com/couch-through-door-calculator) and local retailer furniture-fit tools. “Sofa” and “couch” both appeared; result snippets frequently mention front doors, stairs, elevators, tilt/pivot orientations and delivery.

This is a well-matched but competitive utility query cluster. Results promise orientation or turning behavior that a simple width/height comparison cannot prove. FitWise should compete on a transparent bottleneck-by-bottleneck measurement process and conservative language, not copy unvalidated “best angle” claims. The focused destination is `/access/furniture-route-fit/`; `/will-it-fit/` should be a broader one-item room/space check with a clear handoff to Access for route checks.

### General Room Fit and Dining Tables

US and UK searches for “will furniture fit in my room” and “will dining table fit in my room” return multi-object room planners, one-item room-fit calculators, dedicated dining-table sizing calculators and retail guides. Sampled result titles included [ItemFits Room Fit](https://itemfits.com/fit/room), [ToScale’s dining-table fit page](https://toscale.app/will-my-dining-table-fit/), [AI Room Styler’s furniture spacing calculator](https://airoomstyler.com/tools/furniture-spacing-calculator) and a named 3D room planner, [Will It Fit](https://willitfit.io/). Dining-table calculator snippets commonly promise table shape, seat count and chair/walkway clearance; some also promote simple fixed clearance rules.

`/will-it-fit/` should say that it checks a measured item/space comparison, not a full floor-plan optimizer. `/dining/` should own indoor table/room footprint and chair movement; `/garden/patio-dining-fit/` should own outdoor structure/post spans and patio seating. Do not clone more dining-table size pages. Explain the model's actual inputs and avoid universal “72-inch” or chair-clearance rules without reliable citations.

### Desk and Monitor Fit

“Desk size for two monitors” and “desk size for two 27 inch monitors” returned specific calculators and guides, including [Screen Size Comparison](https://screensizecomparison.org/guides/desk-size-for-dual-monitors/), [WorkChoose](https://workchoose.com/dual-monitor-desk-size-calculator), [Designing Idea](https://designingidea.com/home-office-desk-size-calculator/) and multi-monitor layout tools. Result snippets distinguish screen diagonal from actual panel/base width and mention keyboard, mouse, stand, arm, cables, desk depth and remaining workspace.

The query is highly aligned with `/workspace/desk-size-for-two-27-inch-monitors/`, which already has an exact scenario page. It is not an empty SERP. The differentiation opportunity is to show the actual input dimensions and assumptions, distinguish panel width from diagonal size, include stand/arm and user-selected zones, and link to `/workspace/monitor-arm-compatibility/`. Do not repeat unsupported one-size-fits-all “minimum desk” advice.

### Bedroom Layout and Mattress Sizes

“What size bed fits in a 10x10 room” autocomplete suggested 10x10, 10x11, 10x12, 12x12 and 9x9 variants, plus “can a full size bed fit in a 10x10 room.” “Room size for king bed” SERPs contain floor-plan/layout tools, bed-size references and generic minimum-clearance articles. This supports one useful scenario page, not a page template for every room size.

Market results for “bed/mattress sizes” are visibly localized. Australia results include `King Single`, `Queen`, `King` and `Super King`; NZ results emphasize `Super King`; Ireland results use `Small Double`, `King` and `Super King`; South Africa results use `Three Quarter` and extra-length options; Canada results commonly include `Twin`, `Double`, `Queen`, `King` and `California King`. Representative results include [Sealy Australia](https://www.sealy.com.au/mattress-sizes/), [Mattress Reviews Canada](https://www.mattressreviews.ca/articles/mattress-sizes-canada/), [Sealy New Zealand](https://sealy.co.nz/mattress-sizes/), [DFI Beds Ireland](https://www.dfibeds.com/pages/mattress-sizes-ireland) and [Bed King South Africa](https://www.bedking.co.za/mattress-and-base-sizes.php).

These results establish local search language and a content gap, not one definitive national standard. Some SERP snippets disagree on exact values or blend mattress, base and frame dimensions. Do not copy numbers from snippets. Use current, market-specific manufacturer/retailer primary references and state when sizes vary by brand or frame. Current indexable size references cover US and UK only.

### Appliance Fit and Installation

“Will a fridge fit through a door?” is distinct from “will this fridge fit in the cabinet/opening?” The first is a delivery-route question; the second needs exact cabinet dimensions, doors/handles, door swing and manufacturer ventilation allowances. The sampled SERPs contained both specialist calculators and manufacturer/retailer measurement guides. Useful source examples surfaced in results include [GE refrigerator measurements](https://www.geappliances.com/ge/refrigerators/refrigerator-measurements.htm) and [Home Depot’s Measure First guide](https://www.homedepot.com/hdus/en_US/DTCCOMNEW/fetch/Global_Assets/PDFs/MeasureFirst.pdf).

Map installation and product-specific questions to `/appliances/fridge-fit/`, `/appliances/washer-fit/` and `/appliances/appliance-install-fit/`. Map transport through the door and route to `/access/furniture-route-fit/`. Add contextual links between them. Do not imply the installation checker calculates the delivery path, or use generic fridge airflow allowances instead of the exact manual.

### Pool, Gym, Storage and Garage

- Pool-table SERPs are crowded with calculators and size charts for 6/7/8/9-foot tables and cue lengths. Results include [Omni Calculator](https://www.omnicalculator.com/sports/pool-table-room-size) and many smaller exact-match calculator pages. Target the existing `/game-room/pool-table-fit/` with user-entered table/cue measurements and sourced explanation. Validate any BCA/manufacturer formula before using it. Do not blend pool and snooker table queries or publish a generic clearance formula without authority.
- Power-rack queries mix manufacturer dimensions, home-gym setup guides and specific clearance calculators. Keep `/home-gym/power-rack-room-size/` focused on the actual rack footprint, ceiling, bar/plate/loading space and manufacturer instructions. Avoid a universal “9-foot ceiling is enough” claim.
- Storage SERPs often recommend a unit size by apartment/bedroom contents, while `/storage/what-fits-in-storage/` checks measured footprint capacity. These are adjacent but not identical jobs. Make the existing page clear that it answers “what fits in this measured space”; do not promise a recommended rental size until a sourced unit-size and inventory model exists.
- Garage-fit SERPs include vehicle databases and model-selection tools, as well as general calculators. `/vehicle/garage-fit/` is an honest user-measured check today. Model-specific pages require a maintained vehicle-dimension dataset including mirrors and model-year variation; do not create car landing pages from unsourced specifications.

### Garden, Patio, TV and Monitor Arms

- NZ shed searches surface local suppliers and size charts alongside global shed calculators. `/garden/shed-interior-storage/` addresses items inside a measured shed; `/garden/structure-fit/` addresses whether a structure fits the outdoor footprint. Keep those two intents separate.
- Indoor dining-table SERPs are a dense calculator cluster for recommended table length/diameter, seat count, shape and chair clearance. `/dining/` should focus on whether the user's exact table plus chair-use envelope fits a measured room; avoid duplicating the generic recommended-size calculators without independently sourced seating/clearance rules.
- Greenhouse results include layout/bench/aisle planning and plant-capacity tools. `/garden/greenhouse-layout/` can own measured footprint, staging and aisle fit; do not imply plant yield, ventilation or horticulture advice unless separately supported.
- Ireland patio-dining results skew toward sizing guides, chair clearance and retail purchase advice, with outdoor-fit tools also visible. `/garden/patio-dining-fit/` should explain the table + pulled-out chair envelope and post-to-post clear opening. `/dining/` should remain the indoor dining fit task.
- Hot-tub results combine seating/dimensions, service-panel/cover clearance, structural support and delivery. `/garden/hot-tub-fit/` should distinguish footprint/service access from deck load, electrical preparation and delivery-route feasibility; the latter require the product manual or qualified installation guidance.
- TV-stand results include generic “stand should be wider than TV” rules and shopping guides. `/tv/tv-stand-fit/` should emphasize exact outside screen width, stand/feet footprint and manufacturer mounting data rather than a universal diagonal-to-console-width rule.
- Monitor-arm SERPs focus on clamp thickness, desk edge, weight and VESA compatibility. `/workspace/monitor-arm-compatibility/` is the correct task page. Keep those exact terms in visible content and state which specifications need the manufacturer's manual.

## Cannibalization and Content Architecture

- `/will-it-fit/` and `/access/furniture-route-fit/` both relate to doorway fit. Define a one-item general checker vs a detailed route checker; use reciprocal contextual links, but do not give both pages the same title, primary query and first-screen copy.
- `/appliances/appliance-install-fit/`, `/appliances/fridge-fit/` and `/appliances/washer-fit/` should distinguish general measurement process from product-specific dimensions and final installation clearance. Delivery route should remain an Access task.
- `/workspace/desk-size-guide/`, `/workspace/desk-size-for-two-27-inch-monitors/`, `/workspace/what-fits-on-a-140cm-desk/` and `/workspace/120cm-vs-140cm-desk/` need different answers: general measurement method, 2x27 setup, 140 cm capacity, and the specific width comparison. Avoid repeating the same generic “desk size guide” article on all four.
- `/bedroom/us-bed-size-dimensions/` and `/bedroom/uk-bed-size-dimensions/` should not each claim global “king bed size.” Use the market in title/H1, state mattress vs frame, and link to the bedroom fit tool.
- Hubs such as `/garden/`, `/storage/`, `/vehicle/` and `/game-room/` should route to children and summarize the family. The task-specific child should own the precise fit query.
- Trust pages `/about/`, `/methodology/` and `/methodology/reference-conventions/` should support citations and interpretation, not compete with utility pages for generic calculator terms.
